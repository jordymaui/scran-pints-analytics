Sources, definitions & comparison limits

First 3/7 dates means the publication calendar date plus the following 2/6 dates in the saved API export. The first date can be partial; this is not an exact 72/168-hour comparison. Every date must be present exactly once. Missing windows are excluded, never treated as zero.

Watch hours sum estimated minutes watched divided by 60. Net subscribers sum gains minus losses. Studio cards are rounded since-published observations at different video ages. They stay separate from equal-age comparisons and native test watch-time shares.

Saved history is recovered evidence, not a complete switch log. Combined title and thumbnail tests support conclusions about that pairing only. Uncontrolled comparisons between videos suggest questions; they do not establish causality.
