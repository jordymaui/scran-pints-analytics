Privacy & data use.

This dashboard brings together public social reporting, a YEET partnership view and a public YouTube Studio. Account management and the shared experiment notebook are reserved for the team.

Updated 20 October 2026

Who runs this dashboard

Scran & Pints operates this analytics workspace. For questions about its data use, access or deletion, contact team@scranpints.com. This notice describes this analytics application; other Scran & Pints websites may have their own notices.

Information used by the workspace

  • Team sign-in. Google sign-in through Supabase supplies account identity information, including the account identifier, email address and profile information returned by Google. The application checks the verified Google identity against its list of approved team email addresses.
  • Reporting connections. An approved team member can separately authorize read-only YouTube, Instagram and TikTok reporting. The server stores reporting grants, account identifiers, granted permissions and connection dates so it can refresh reports. Platform passwords are entered with the platform, not this application.
  • Channel and content reports. The application uses YouTube API Services to read video metadata and available performance reports, including views, watch time, engagement, subscriber changes, traffic and retention. Each report retains its source and reporting dates. Other social platforms have separate connection requirements and may have no report available.
  • Workshop records. The team notebook stores unpublished hypotheses, proposed packaging, experiment notes and decisions. Studio displays selected saved reporting snapshots and historical title/thumbnail results for comparison.

How the information is used and shared

We use these records to review our content, compare results, maintain dated reporting and plan improvements. YouTube reporting access is read-only; the collectors do not publish videos or change titles, descriptions, tags or thumbnails.

Public YouTube reporting includes Data API counters and metadata for public videos, alongside selected channel and Shorts performance from YouTube Analytics: available aggregate and daily views, watch hours, likes, comments, shares and subscriber changes. Each series shows its own reporting window and collection date. Some metrics are available only as period totals; unavailable daily values remain unavailable. Where current reporting is unavailable, the application can show the separately labelled, previously published historical snapshot.

When connected, public Instagram reporting includes available post counters, current followers, native daily account views and aggregated follower demographics. TikTok reporting includes current followers and public video titles, publication dates, views, likes, comments and shares returned by its Display API. This TikTok connection does not supply watch time, historical daily activity or demographics. Platform reports retain separate dates and definitions; counts do not represent unique people across platforms.

The dashboard, Studio, Connections status and report downloads can be read without signing in, including by YEET. Studio includes episode performance, retention, aggregated audience and demographic breakdowns, public video metadata and recovered title/thumbnail test results. Connections publishes connection and collection status. Credentials, sign-in identities, raw source files, unpublished notebook entries and account-management actions remain restricted to approved team accounts. The repository also contains a separately published historical archive that was intentionally made public; copies already shared outside this application are not controlled by a sign-out or connection change.

Hosting, cookies and connected services

Vercel hosts the site and server functions. Supabase provides team authentication and private database storage. Google processes sign-in and YouTube API requests. Meta and TikTok process their own account authorizations and reporting requests when connected. These services process information needed to deliver those features.

The application uses session and security cookies to maintain team sign-in and complete account connections. Reporting credentials stay on the server. Some public video thumbnails load from YouTube image servers, which receive the browser requests needed to display them. Following an external platform link takes you to that platform's service.

Workshop exports and public report downloads save files to the device you choose. The workshop can import earlier browser drafts when a team member chooses that action.

Access, storage and deletion requests

Saved reports and their prior versions support historical comparisons. A failed refresh can leave the previous report in place with its earlier date. This application does not currently provide an automatic deletion schedule or a self-service deletion button.

To request deletion of a connection, stored reports or workshop information, email team@scranpints.com and identify the account, channel or records concerned. Requests are handled by the team; do not include passwords or access tokens in your message. Deleting records held by this application does not delete videos or account data held by YouTube.

You can separately revoke the application's Google access in your Google Account's third-party connections. Revoking access prevents future authorized collection; it does not itself remove stored records from this application. Contact the team to request their deletion.

Instagram and TikTok access can also be revoked in the connected account's apps and permissions settings. Revoking access stops future authorized collection; previously stored reporting is retained until the team processes a deletion request.

Google and YouTube information

This application uses YouTube API Services. Read the Google Privacy Policy, YouTube Terms of Service and YouTube API Services Developer Policies for those services' terms and data practices.

A question about your data? Email team@scranpints.com.
