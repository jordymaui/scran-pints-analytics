Where the numbers come from

This is all the used or tested material we have recovered, not a guaranteed complete YouTube history. Drafts are excluded. Repeated title captures are grouped; repeated images are grouped by file match or a checked visual match. The original captures remain in the saved records.

Whole-video CTR, reach and watch hours are displayed on every card. Cards from the same video repeat that video’s totals, so do not add them together. Separate version-level CTR, reach and watch hours are not supplied by the saved comparison results. Video age and audience can affect the numbers.

Test share means the percentage of that test’s watch time earned by a title-and-image combination. Compare shares only within the same test. An unfinished test or a result with no clear winner stays unproven. A selected version is not automatically a winner.
