Using the workspace

Scran & Pints operates this tool for public reporting and the team's internal content research. Private reports, connections and notes are available only to approved team accounts and expressly approved agents. The application currently enforces an exact team-email allowlist.

The YouTube connection is read-only. Research notes and suggested packaging are planning records; they do not make changes on YouTube. Information about account access and stored data is in our privacy notice.

Reading the reports

Metrics come from dated platform reports or labelled historical records. Platforms may revise counts, delay reports or withhold unavailable metrics. Check each source's coverage, capture date and definitions before comparing results. Different platforms' views do not represent a deduplicated audience.

Channel performance does not, by itself, prove sponsor exposure, conversions or return on investment. Campaign claims require their own attribution and evidence. This tool does not guarantee financial or campaign results.

Platform services

The application uses YouTube API Services. Use of those features is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and applicable YouTube API Services Terms of Service. Google's services have their own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
