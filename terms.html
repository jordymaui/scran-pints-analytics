Using the workspace

Scran & Pints operates this tool for public reporting and the team's internal content research. The dashboard, Studio reports and Connections status are public to view. Public Refresh controls can collect reports from the connected Scran & Pints accounts without sign-in, subject to server cooldowns and platform availability. Account connection changes and the shared experiment notebook require an approved team account. These management actions use an exact team-email allowlist.

The YouTube, Instagram and TikTok reporting connections are read-only. Research notes and suggested packaging are planning records; they do not change platform content. Information about account access and stored data is in our privacy notice.

Reading the reports

Metrics come from dated platform reports or labelled historical records. Public YouTube reports include selected channel and Shorts aggregate and daily performance, with separate dates for public lifetime counters. A daily performance value is activity for that reporting date; a lifetime counter is a total observed at capture. Unavailable daily metrics are not reconstructed from period totals. Shorts are included in all-format channel performance and must not be added to those totals.

Platforms may revise counts, delay reports or withhold unavailable metrics. Check each source's coverage, capture date and definitions before comparing results. Different platforms' views do not represent a deduplicated audience.

Channel performance does not, by itself, prove sponsor exposure, conversions or return on investment. Campaign claims require their own attribution and evidence. This tool does not guarantee financial or campaign results.
