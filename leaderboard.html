What the score means

Score = observed watch-time share. It is the percentage of a comparison's watch time earned by that option. Rows are ordered numerically, highest first. The comparisons had different viewers, dates and competing options, so this is a useful reference table, not a controlled overall ranking of title or thumbnail quality.

We show the latest scored result available for each row, not its personal best. Unfinished earlier snapshots are labelled beside their score. Results from completed comparisons here had no clear winner. Open any row's evidence to see its dates, paired image and older observations.

Scores belong to the tested title-and-thumbnail pairing, not each element independently. No individual CTR has been invented. No-score entries sit below the numbered places and are not treated as zero or as losers. Equal scores share a place; filtering preserves overall positions.

Thumbnail rows group matching recovered files. Earlier screenshot crops remain separate evidence records where a matching design is unverified. The historical snapshot was captured on 4 October 2026; this is not a live feed of newly launched tests.
