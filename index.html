Data sources, definitions & what is still missing

This is a dated snapshot, not a live feed. API inventory and performance captured 4 October 2026; processed daily metrics currently end 1 October. Studio snapshots have their own displayed ranges and may be newer.

First 3 / 7 calendar dates include the partial publication day in YouTube's Pacific timezone. They are not exact 72-hour / 168-hour windows. Incomplete windows display “Not ready”; they are not treated as zero. Reach and CTR are hidden in these comparisons until matching window data is available.

Watch hours = reported estimated minutes watched ÷ 60. Average view duration is Google's returned metric, not recomputed from total views. Views, engaged views and differently processed panels can have different denominators. We do not force them to reconcile.

The near-30-second value uses the closest of 100 duration buckets, not the exact Studio intro metric. Audience watch ratio can include rewatches and is not a unique-viewer survival probability.

Packaging history includes only recovered evidence. “Thumbnail missing” and “variant metrics unavailable” are real gaps, not zero results. Draft ideas are not treated as uploaded packages. Native tests may expose watch-time share without variant CTR or impressions.

Automatic bulk reach reports are pending. The Google connection is still in Testing mode; ongoing unattended refresh is not yet guaranteed. No titles, thumbnails or published videos were changed to build this page.
